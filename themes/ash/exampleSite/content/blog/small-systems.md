A boundary tells the reader where a system begins and ends. It also makes the responsibilities around the system easier to discuss.

## Start small

Choose one task whose result can be inspected. Write down its inputs, outputs, and exceptions.

## Keep the feedback

The smallest useful system still needs a way to report when its assumptions no longer hold.
