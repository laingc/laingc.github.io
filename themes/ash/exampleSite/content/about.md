The masthead uses Christopher Laing’s Integrated cadence identity. Alex Morgan is a fictional author for the sample essays. The example content describes the theme rather than a real person’s career.

Ash is an editorial design language built around warm paper, graphite type, and a restrained terracotta accent. Its dark mode uses warm charcoal, ivory, and copper.

{{< figure src="images/landscape.jpg" alt="A mountain range beyond a lake under a cloud-filled sky." caption="The same photograph receives a restrained colour treatment in each mode." >}}

The theme supports an introductory homepage, a writing archive, and general pages like this one. The content lives in Markdown files and Hugo configuration.
